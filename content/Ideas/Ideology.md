> "We all tell lies and hide our true feelings, for complete free expression is a social impossibility."[^2]

- people's values and morals are often very emotionally charged. Which is why they take offence when they are challenged by other people's novel and unorthodox ideas.
- this just shows that ideology is dangerous. It's hard to change, even when it's wrong
- the black sheep is ostracized for a reason
- every society needs a person who goes against the grain and criticizes the 'common way'. So sometimes it works in your advantage to outwardly present your novel ideas. 
	- often this is the role of the comedian. I remember Jordan Peterson and Andrew Schultz talking about this.
- *"You must have two faces. Show one to the crowd, and keep the other for yourself and your Creator. If you want to keep your eyes, your ears and your tongue, forget that you have them."*[^3]
	- you must be very careful how you present yourself in public. Some things are better left in your own mind

## Separate Yourself from Ideology
- The rational mind that produces an idea and worships it as if its absolute, immediately occupies hell[^4]
	- This is what happened with communism
- ideology should never be taken as absolute truth because NOTHING is absolute truth

## Suffering and Ideology
- those who are suffering, which is quite frankly most of the world, are most vulnerable to ideology.
- ideologies like Communism preach that suffering is an anomaly and NOT part of the human condition, and then they try to solve them in some way which often doesn't work[^5]
