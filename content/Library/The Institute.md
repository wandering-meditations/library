# Summary
- Follows the story of a young boy who is taken to the 'Institute', a place full of other 'gifted' kids. These kids all have either telepathic or telekinetic abilities
- there are different levels in this Institute. The kids don't really know what happens in the other levels, they assume eventually you'll be let free
- they end up finding out that when you proceed to the next level, your mind slowly withers away and you eventually die
- The leaders of the Institute are using these kids to harness their mental abilities in order to commit murders of high-status people (like politicians) around the world
	- the way it works is they show a video to the kids involving that person, and the kids all hold hands and think about it. They don't actually know what they're doing though.
- Eventually the main character escapes and gets help and frees the kids. They also find out that there are multiple Institutes around the world.
