### Non-Fiction
[[12 Rules for Life]] by Jordan Peterson
- all around terrific self-help book that is based on hard evidence. Very well researched, clear, and easy to understand.

[[A History of Religious Ideas Vol 1]] by Mircea Eliade
- super detailed and comprehensive overview of major religious themes. Nothing about this book is sub-par

[[Afghanistan - A Cultural and Political History]] by Thomas Barfield
- I've read several books on Afghan history and this is the best one
