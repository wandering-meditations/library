#### Non-Fiction

[[Afghanistan - A Military History from Alexander The Great to the War Against the Taliban]]
- thorough history of Afghanistan from a war/military perspective

[[Afghanistan - A Cultural and Political History]]
- this is the best book to read if you want a comprehensive overview of Afghanistan at a very in-depth level. Covers history, politics, and culture.

[[Afghanistan - Graveyard of Empires]]
- general overview of Afghan history; very high-level / basic

[[Pukhtun - An Insight]]
- focuses on the culture and history of the Pukhtun/Pashtun people

[[The Afghanistan Papers - A Secret History of the War]]
- in-depth history of the US invasion of Afghanistan (2001-2021)

[[The Return of the Taliban]]
- overview of the "new Taliban", 2021-present
